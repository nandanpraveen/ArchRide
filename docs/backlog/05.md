# [Week 2] Building-to-building planner and bus-versus-walking comparison

## Summary

Let a student choose an origin and destination and compare **walking directly** with a **complete single-bus journey**. Recommend only buses they can actually reach before boarding.

## Background

This uses task 2’s route patterns/stop occurrences, task 4’s boarding locations, and task 3’s walking/buffer preferences. Put the calculation behind a typed, testable planning module and expose it through Next.js server code. Agree estimate/freshness interfaces with task 6 before implementing ranking.

This is a **Week 2** task, from **2026-10-18 to 2026-10-25**, with the Sunday handoff in **America/New_York**. Claim the draft in the project before starting.

## Requirements

- [ ] Accept current location, building/entrance, stop, or selected map point as origin; search verified buildings/stops for a destination. Preserve usable manual inputs when GPS is denied.
- [ ] Choose and verify an authorized walking-routing provider or campus pedestrian graph. Use actual pedestrian paths and known entrances, preserve attribution, cache by locations/preferences with expiry, and document limits. A straight-line distance cannot be labeled a verified walk.
- [ ] Generate direct single-bus candidates using the ordered route pattern, correct direction, and distinct stop occurrences. Include walking to board, waiting after arrival at the stop, riding, and walking from the exit to the destination.
- [ ] Exclude buses whose boarding time is earlier than the user’s estimated stop arrival plus the safety buffer. Rank supported candidates against direct walking using total travel time and the chosen walking preference.
- [ ] Show complete journey steps, route/direction, boarding and exit stops, total duration, destination arrival, amount of walking, estimate provenance, and a plain-language recommendation.
- [ ] Implement leave-now planning and a typed arrival-deadline input consumed by task 6. Recalculate when the query, preference, or current data changes; never reuse expired bus selections.
- [ ] Expose unsupported journeys, missing walking paths, insufficient/stale arrivals, no active service, and outside-campus inputs honestly, with a valid walk-only option where available.
- [ ] Wire Plan into Home search, route/stop details, and saved-trip/class entry points. Use accessible Material origin/destination fields and results.
- [ ] `pnpm lint`, `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm test`, and `pnpm build` pass. Add meaningful feature tests, including the cases below.

## Out of scope

- Multi-bus transfers, off-campus general transit planning, or unverified accessible-route claims.
- Invented pedestrian shortcuts through buildings or closed areas.

## What we'll test

- Every displayed total equals the walk + wait + ride + final walk; waiting starts after the student reaches the stop.
- A bus just reachable, exactly at the safety threshold, already departed, and unreachable even though its displayed ETA is short.
- Identical origin/destination, one-way loops, repeated stops, opposite direction, route variants, no direct bus, and campus boundaries.
- Walking faster, bus faster, equal-time ties, preferred less walking, missing entrance, path-provider outage, and cache expiry.
- Reversed inputs, rapid edits/out-of-order responses, midnight service, denied GPS, and saved-trip recalculation with different live vehicles.
