# ArchRide

## Register

product

## Users

UGA students checking a phone outdoors between classes, often in bright light and with little time, or planning their campus day on a laptop. Travel screens require Google or email/password sign-in. Schedules, saved trips and preferences stay in the current browser, separated by account.

## Product Purpose

Turn campus transit data into a useful choice: walk or take a direct bus, where to board, when to leave, and whether an arrival deadline looks achievable. The full agreed scope is in [the app brief](docs/APP_BRIEF.md).

## Brand Personality

Clear, practical, calm. Use Material Design interaction patterns and a restrained campus red, black, and neutral palette. State estimates plainly and identify uncertainty.

## Anti-references

Avoid crowded dashboards full of unexplained bus markers, decorative landing pages, unsupported live countdowns, and accuracy or official UGA endorsement claims.

## Design Principles

- Make the next useful action easy to find.
- Show complete journeys, including both walks and the wait.
- Expose freshness, provenance, outages, and uncertainty.
- Keep sign-in simple; location permission remains optional and manual origins remain available.
- Keep personal schedules and saved trips private.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA, keyboard operation, screen-reader labels, text alongside route colors, reduced motion, comfortable touch targets, and usable layouts at 320 px. Location is optional; manual origins must be supported. Do not claim walking paths are wheelchair accessible without verified path data.
